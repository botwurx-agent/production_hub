# Studio Flows tour film: beat sheet

File: `studio-flows-tour.mp4` (1920x1080, 30fps, 75.5s, no audio).
Made to sit under or beside you talking. Every beat is one move on screen and
one line at the foot of the frame, so your voice can follow it or ignore it.

The right column is a suggested line to say over each beat. It is written to
land inside the beat's length at a normal speaking pace. Change any of it.

## Notes for the edit

- The lines on the film are designed captions, not a transcript. Once your
  voice is recorded, real subtitles of what you say go on YOUR half of the
  split screen, so the two never fight.
- The bottom 30% of the film is reserved for its caption. If your layout
  crops the film, crop the top or sides, not the foot.
- No music or sound is on the file.
- To change a line, a timing or a shot: `components/marketing/tour-film.tsx`,
  then `node scripts/film/render-tour.mjs` with the dev server running.
